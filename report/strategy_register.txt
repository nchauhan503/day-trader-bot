STRATEGY REGISTER

Every strategy is written down here before it is run. A rule may not change
after its first result. A change means a new entry in this file with a new
name, tested on its own.

Common to all strategies
  Universe   the 102 S&P 100 names in fund_pit.UNIVERSE that have 5-minute data
  Data       5-minute bars, the session's regular hours, 9:30 to 4:00 ET
  Delay      entry is the open of the bar that starts 15 minutes after the
             signal bar ends, plus $0.01 slippage. This is the test that matters.
  Exits      stop, target, breakeven rule (where stated), 120-minute time stop,
             or 15:50 ET, whichever comes first. Stops fill at min(open, stop)
             minus slippage. If a bar touches both stop and target, the stop wins.
  Sizing     none. Results are in R: (exit - fill) / (fill - stop).
  Trades     one per name per session, the first signal that qualifies.
  Direction  long only (the live system is long only).

S1  orb_volume   (registered 2026-11-27)

Source: sam-bateman/trading-orb (GitHub), with the parts that do not apply
to a long-only, 2-hour system removed. Its results are self-reported.

  Opening range   high and low of the first four 5-minute bars (9:30 to 9:50 ET).
                  Width W = high - low.
  Signal          the first 5-minute bar, ending between 10:00 and 11:30 ET,
                  whose close is above the opening-range high, and no earlier
                  bar in the session closed above it.
  Volume filter   the signal bar's volume is at least 1.2 times the average
                  volume of the previous 10 bars.
  Stop            fill - 0.50 W
  Target          fill + 0.75 W
  Breakeven       once a bar's high reaches fill + W, the stop moves up to
                  the fill price. It never moves down.
  Skip            if the target is already at or below the entry bar's open.

Deliberate changes from the source, and why:
  - long only, because the live system is long only
  - a 120-minute time stop instead of the close, because the goal is a 1-2 hour hold
  - the 15-minute entry delay, which the source does not model

Forward test for S1 (registered 2026-11-27, before the first S1 forward row)
  Starts 2026-11-30, the first session after this entry.
  Logged each session by forward.py under strategy "S1". Triggers only,
  no alerts, same delay and exits as above.
  PASS only if, over all S1 forward sessions:
    1. mean R > 0
    2. t >= 2
    3. mean R > 0 in the first half of the sessions
    4. mean R > 0 in the second half
  Otherwise REJECT.
  S1 is one of several strategies being tracked. A pass on one of them is
  weaker evidence than a pass on one, so the number of strategies tested is
  reported alongside any result.
